#### 核心功能
Flash OS images to SD cards & USB drives, safely and easily.  把系统镜像安全且简单地刷到SD卡和U盘中

#### 网站
官网: https://etcher.balena.io/

开源地址: https://github.com/balena-io/etcher

#### 支持的操作系统 Supported Operating Systems
- Linux; most distros; Intel 64-bit.
- Windows 10 and later; Intel 64-bit.
- macOS 10.13 (High Sierra) and later; both Intel and Apple Silicon.

#### 我的使用场景
- 刷PVE到U盘
