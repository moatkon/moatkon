# 43. Changing a Flat Tire <small>更换瘪的轮胎</small>
My car had a flat tire,so I had to change it.
- I **jacked**`千斤顶` the car up.
- I took off the flat tire...
- ...and took the spare`备用` tire out of the trunk`箱`.
- I put the spare on the wheel,...
- tightened the **lug nuts**`凸耳螺母` by hand,...
- ...lowered the car to the ground,...
- ...and tightened the nuts some more with a **lug wrench**`单向扳手`.
- Finally,I put the hubcup`轮毂` on the wheel.

# 44. Dealing with a Power Failure <small>处理电源故障</small>
- During a thunderstorm...
- ...our power went out.
- I turned on a flashnight...
- ...and my sister lit some candles.
- We found the **circuit-breaker box**`断路器盒`...
- ...and found the breakers that had tripped.
- We reset the breakers...
- ...and the power came back on.

# 45. Working with Wood
- Before you cut a board,you measure it...
- ...and mark the places to cut.
- Then you cut it with a saw.
- **To trim**`修剪` a piece of wood,you plane`刨` it.
- To make the edges smooth,you sand them.
- To join two pieces of wood,you can glue them together,...
- ...nail`钉` them together...
- ...or join them with screws`螺丝钉`.
